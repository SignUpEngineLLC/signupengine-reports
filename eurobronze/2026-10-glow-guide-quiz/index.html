The biggest opportunity

Turn every quiz result into a client you can reach again

Keep the result and the Book button exactly as they are, and add one optional offer underneath: “Email me my Glow Guide plus $10 off my first tan.” People who are ready to book can still book right away, and people who aren’t ready yet leave their email so you can follow up.

Why it pays off

  • Builds a client list EuroBronze owns. Unlike ad traffic, it keeps working when ads are paused, and it grows with every quiz.
  • Reaches people who leave without booking. 405 people have seen a result and left, and right now there’s no way to contact them.
  • Gives new clients a reason to book now. A discount with an expiration date encourages people who are interested to book their first appointment before it runs out.
  • Makes repeat business easy. A sunless tan lasts about a week, so rebooking reminders and seasonal offers can go to people who already know their shade.
  • Every email can be personal. The quiz already knows each person’s service, occasion and timing, so follow-ups can speak to brides, event guests and regulars differently.
  • Makes future ad spend go further. When ads restart, they can be shown to people on the list, who already know EuroBronze and their recommended tan.

Next steps

Recommendations

  1. Finding

    Ads doubled quiz traffic and added about 8 book clicks a week

    Action

    Restart digital ads pointing to the Glow Guide, ideally with the email offer in place so every ad click can join the client list. Fix the quiz’s traffic-source tracking first so ad results can be measured directly.

  2. Finding

    405 people saw a result and left with no way to follow up

    Action

    Add the optional “Email me my Glow Guide plus $10 off” offer under the Book button, then follow up with everyone who signs up.

  3. Finding

    Red Carpet is now the most common result but books the least (35%)

    Action

    Test a Red Carpet bundle or a time-limited incentive and compare its rate over the next 30 days.

  4. Finding

    People who choose contouring or add-ons book most often

    Action

    Show the person’s chosen extras on the result page and in their booking, and bundle Glow Guard or Finishing Powder into first-visit offers.
