Tennis 101 Program Report

Adult beginner program at the USTA Midwest Tennis Center, July and October 2026 sessions

Prepared for USTA Midwest Tennis Center
Orders through 30 September 2026

At a glance

  1. Three class times carry the program. Weekend mornings and Monday/Wednesday 6 PM account for 7 in 10 enrollments in both sessions.
  2. Bring-a-friend is the main growth driver. 62% of players came in as part of a free friend pair, mostly friends rather than couples.
  3. The core audience is women aged 25 to 44. Women make up 70% of players, and most of the men come on weekends.
  4. Players drive in from the north. Half live in Hamilton County, Zionsville or north Indianapolis, and the median trip is about 9 miles.
  5. Every October player is new. None of the 69 July players re-enrolled in Tennis 101.

Most popular class times

Weekend mornings and Monday/Wednesday evenings fill up. Weekday daytime classes draw about half as many.

Enrollments by class

  • The three most popular classes reached 16 or more players in both sessions.
  • Each time slot draws a different player. Monday/Wednesday 6 PM is 85% women with a median age of 32. Weekday midday classes skew older, with a median of 43. The 7 AM class is the youngest, at 27.
  • Weekend mornings bring in 25 of the 42 men across both sessions.
  • Tuesday/Thursday 1:30 PM has one October registration so far.

Audience

Seven in ten players are women, and two in three are aged 25 to 44.

Players by age and gender, both sessions (138 people)

  • The median age is 36, and men and women are about the same age.
  • One in four players is 45 or older.
  • The share of women rose from 67% in July to 72% in October.

Player progression

None of the 69 July players signed up for October Tennis 101, so every October enrollment is a new player.

That is what a beginner program should look like. This export can't show whether July graduates moved into another USTA Midwest program, but matching them against Club Automation enrollments would show how many moved up.

Opportunities to consider

Schedule

  • If court time and coaches allow, more weekend morning and Monday/Wednesday evening classes would likely fill.
  • The Tuesday/Thursday 1:30 PM class may be worth rethinking.
  • A waitlist on the most popular classes would capture people who miss out and show how much demand is going unmet. The new SignUpEngine waitlist handles this automatically, notifying people when a spot opens.

Marketing

  • Lead with bring-a-friend, and feature pairs of friends in the creative.
  • Focus ads on women aged 25 to 44 within about 15 miles, weighted toward Carmel, Westfield, Fishers, Zionsville and north Indianapolis.
  • Use weekend class times in creative aimed at men.

Growth

  • Invite graduating friend pairs to move into the next level together.
  • Track July graduates in Club Automation to see how many moved up.

About the data

Based on 139 enrollments from 96 orders in the SignUpEngine order export, placed between 11 June and 30 September 2026, where the program is labeled Indianapolis LTN. October registration is still open, so October figures are to date. Revenue is the amount paid after the friend discount. Ages and gender come from each registration. Location is based on billing zip code, measured as straight-line distance to the center on East 96th Street, so actual travel distances are longer; five enrollments with unrecognized or out-of-state zip codes are excluded from distance figures. One October player appears in two orders for the same class, one of them at no charge, and is counted once in player figures.
