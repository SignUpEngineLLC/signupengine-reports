Tennis Now Program Report

Adult and junior beginner programs at the Cary Leeds Center, July to October 2026

Prepared for NYJTL
Data through 23 September 2026

At a glance

  1. Adult demand is outpacing supply. Every September/October adult class sold out, most within two days, a full month before classes began.
  2. The core adult audience is women aged 25 to 44. Women make up 81% of adult players.
  3. Weekend mornings and 8 PM weeknights sell fastest. The weekday morning class was the slowest to fill.
  4. Most players live within 5 miles, mainly in the Bronx and upper Manhattan.
  5. Juniors are a different market. Families book weeks ahead rather than days, and five of six classes filled.

Adult audience

Four in five adult players are women, and three in four are aged 25 to 44.

Adult players by age and gender, both sessions (93 people)

  • The median age is 36, the same in both sessions.
  • One in five is 45 or older, and most of them are women.
  • Men are younger, with a median age of 32.5, and their share fell from 24% in July/August to 13% in September/October.

Most popular class times

Weekend mornings and 8 PM weeknights sell out fastest.

Time for each September/October adult class to sell out

  • Five of the six September/October classes sold out in under two days. Sunday 8 AM was added once the others began filling, which helps explain its speed.
  • Thursday 10 AM, the only weekday daytime class, took more than five days, though it did fill.
  • In July/August, the two weekend 2 PM classes filled fastest. Sunday 1 PM was the only adult class that never filled.

At every level, the weekday 5 PM class filled faster than the Saturday class.

  • 43 children from 38 families: 23 girls and 20 boys, median age 8. Five families enrolled siblings.
  • 84% live within 5 miles. Riverdale and Kingsbridge sent the most children (9), followed by the club's own neighborhood (6).
  • Families typically booked about three weeks before classes began.
  • Only one of the 38 families also has a parent in adult Tennis Now.

Player progression

9 of the 55 July/August adults (16%) repeated the beginner program in September/October instead of moving up a level.

Because they registered early, these players took 7 of the first 17 September/October seats, ahead of new beginners. It suggests players are not being moved into a next level at the end of each session, or that one is not available at the right time.

Opportunities to consider

Meet the demand

  • If court time and coaches allow, more adult classes at 8 PM on weeknights or on weekend mornings would likely fill.
  • A waitlist on sold-out classes would capture people who miss out and show how much demand is going unmet. The new SignUpEngine waitlist handles this automatically, notifying people when a spot opens.

Target precisely

  • Focus adult marketing on women aged 25 to 44 within 5 miles, including upper Manhattan.
  • Start junior promotion earlier and keep it running until classes begin.

Grow the program

  • Invite junior parents to adult classes, ideally at the same time as their children's.
  • Encourage graduates to move up a level before each session ends, freeing beginner seats for new players.
  • A bring-a-partner offer could help attract more men.

About the data

Based on 146 enrollments in the Tennis Now order export, July to September 2026, at $99 each. Ages and gender come from each registration. Location is based on billing zip code, measured as straight-line distance to 1720 Crotona Avenue, so actual travel distances are longer. Four adults with out-of-state billing addresses are excluded from distance figures.
