About the Presentation

Stand for the Silent was started in 2010 by a group of high school students in Oklahoma City who had heard what happened to Ty. His parents, Kirk and Laura, made the students’ cause their life’s work, and Kirk has been visiting schools ever since.

Kirk simply tells students what happened to his family. They come away understanding that what they say and do to each other matters, and that each of them matters too.

About 90 seconds

Each Presentation Includes

Student VolunteersThree students from your school help lead the program, reading from scripts we provide. We ask them to arrive 20 to 30 minutes early.
Five Empty ChairsEach chair stands for a child who was bullied and died by suicide. The volunteers read each child’s story, written from the parents’ point of view. The fifth is Ty’s.
Kirk’s StoryKirk talks about what his family has lived through, and about kindness and the worth of every person. Students also watch a short video made by other students.
The PledgeStudents read “I Am Somebody” together, and anyone who wants to take the pledge receives a pledge card.
Time for QuestionsKirk answers questions and stays afterward to meet students.

A note for administrators

The presentation talks openly about suicide and loss, in words written for students. Many schools let families know ahead of time and have counselors available that day. We’re glad to talk through what’s right for your school.
