Timing

New families by month

New families peaked in August (36), then averaged about four a week in September and October. In the fall, a somewhat larger share stated a rating or level (43% vs 26%) and said they live outside the DC area (25% vs 17%). The fall group is small (28 families), so these differences could be chance.

Related changes

Related changes in the new page mockup

  1. Finding

    Families ask for an evaluation or tryout

    New page

    In the mockup, the evaluation request is in the first screen, with the option to send a video or visit College Park.

  2. Finding

    Families add ratings, ages and phone numbers to their messages

    New page

    In the mockup, the form includes fields for phone, age, UTR or USTA level, and an optional video link.

  3. Finding

    The current form does not ask where the player lives

    New page

    In the mockup, the form asks first whether the player is based in the US or internationally, then for city and country.

  4. Finding

    Families ask which program fits

    New page

    In the mockup, program cards list ages and levels side by side, and the Champs I and II descriptions match their levels.
